# Clenzy Lite Privacy Policy

Clenzy Lite lets you review photos and videos from your Apple Photos library. The app does not collect personal data, use analytics or advertising SDKs, or send your media to the developer.

## Photos access

Clenzy Lite uses the Photos access you grant to display and sort media you can review. You can give access to selected items or your full library and change that access in Settings. If an item is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple Photos may fetch it for viewing or playback under your iCloud settings.

Clenzy Lite keeps your review position, starred items, preferences, undo history, and pending Trash selections in its local app storage. It asks Apple Photos to remove the items you submit from the Trash queue after you confirm the request. Apple Photos manages Recently Deleted and any changes that sync through iCloud Photos.

Deleting Clenzy Lite removes its local review data. You can manage your media, including Recently Deleted, in Apple Photos.

For privacy questions, visit the [Clenzy Lite support page](support.md).
