# Clenzy Lite for Android Privacy Policy

Clenzy Lite uses the Photos and Videos access you grant to show and review media in Android’s gallery. You can change its access in Android Settings. Optional media management access allows supported Trash and restore operations without repeated Android prompts.

Your photos and videos are reviewed on the device. Clenzy Lite has no account, ads, or analytics, and does not send your media or review activity to the developer. Your review position, starred items, recent swipe actions, and preferences are saved in local app storage. You can clear that data in Android Settings or by uninstalling Clenzy Lite. Android manages the retention and recovery of media moved to its Trash.

For support or privacy questions, [contact the Clenzy Lite project](https://github.com/skyemain/VCleaner/issues/new). Do not include private photos or videos in a public issue.
