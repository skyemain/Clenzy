# Clenzy for Android Privacy Policy

Clenzy reviews photos, videos, and shared files on your Android device. You choose what it can access using Android permissions and what to remove or compress. Clenzy cannot access other apps’ private files.

Clenzy uses Photos and Videos access to browse, compare, and review your media. All files access, if enabled, lets it look for downloads and cleanup candidates across shared folders, including files that are not indexed as photos or videos. Media management access is optional and lets Android process supported actions without repeatedly asking for confirmation. You can revoke these permissions in Android Settings.

Clenzy analyzes and compresses your media on the device. It does not send your photos, videos, file contents, or recognition results to a Clenzy server. Review choices, cleanup history, settings and scan results are stored in the app’s local storage. You can clear that data in Android Settings or by uninstalling Clenzy. Android manages the retention and recovery of media sent to its Trash.

Clenzy includes Google ML Kit. Google documents that its Android SDKs can transmit device and app information, installation identifiers, performance metrics, feature usage and error information for diagnostics and usage analytics over HTTPS. For details, see [Google’s ML Kit data disclosures](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

For support or privacy questions, [contact the Clenzy project](https://github.com/skyemain/VCleaner/issues/new). Do not include private files in a public issue.
