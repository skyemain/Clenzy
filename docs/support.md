# Clenzy Lite Support

Clenzy Lite reviews photos and videos from Apple Photos. Swipe right to move on, swipe left to queue an item for Trash, and use Undo to reverse either of your two most recent swipes. Tap the Trash button and confirm the batch when prompted by Apple Photos.

Long-press a photo or video to open Options. To check permissions, choose Settings → Access check. You can change Clenzy Lite's Photos access in your device settings.

If you already confirmed a deletion, open Recently Deleted in Apple Photos to check for the item.

For help or to report a bug, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/skyemain/VCleaner/issues/new). Include your device model, iOS version, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Keep personal photos and videos out of public reports.
